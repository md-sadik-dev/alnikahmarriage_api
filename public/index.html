Our Services

Nikah Services

Free Nikah Service for poor people (below poverty line) as per Shariya.

Hidden Nikah Services

For those who lives in live in relationship, to protect them from (haram) activities we offer nikah according to Shariya to purify their relation. (chargeable)

Talaq Service

As per Shariya Law we provide Talaq service in presence of ULMA IKRAM. (Free Service)

Consultancy & Counselling Services

To protect muslim marriage from miss understanding or un compatibility we provide free counselling and free consultancy to give one more changes to their marriage life.

Khula Service

If female is not happy with their husband and after counselling she is not willing to continue her relation with her husband then we provide KHULA service in presence of ULMA IKRAM as per Shariyat Law.(Free Service)
